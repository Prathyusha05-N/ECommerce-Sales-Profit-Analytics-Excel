# E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, and payment patterns.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed using PivotTables, PivotCharts, calculated fields, and slicers to help users explore business performance dynamically.

## Dataset

- Total Orders: 8,000
- Total Units Sold: 15,294
- Time Period: January 2025 – December 2025
- Product Categories: Electronics, Home & Kitchen, Fashion, Beauty, Sports
- Customer Segments: Consumer, Corporate, Small Business
- Sales Channels: Website, Mobile App, Marketplace

## Data Preparation

The raw e-commerce dataset was converted into an Excel Table named `Sales_Data`.

Calculated columns were created to derive important business metrics:

- Gross Sales
- Discount Amount
- Net Sales
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- Year
- Month
- Month Number
- Quarter

### Key Calculations

**Gross Sales**

`Quantity × Unit Price`

**Discount Amount**

`Gross Sales × Discount`

**Net Sales**

`Gross Sales − Discount Amount`

**Profit**

`Net Sales − Cost`

**Profit Margin**

`Profit ÷ Net Sales`

## Dashboard KPIs

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides analysis of:

### Monthly Sales Trend
Tracks sales performance across the 12 months of 2025.

### Sales by Category
Compares sales generated by each product category.

### Sales by Region
Identifies the strongest-performing geographical regions.

### Top 10 Products
Highlights the products generating the highest sales.

### Customer Segment Analysis
Compares Consumer, Corporate, and Small Business customers.

### Payment Method Analysis
Analyzes order distribution across different payment methods.

## Interactive Features

The dashboard includes slicers for:

- Region
- Category
- Customer Segment
- Sales Channel

These slicers allow users to dynamically filter the dashboard and analyze specific business segments.

## Key Business Insights

- Electronics was the strongest-performing product category.
- The South region recorded the highest regional sales.
- Smartphones were among the highest-performing products.
- Consumer customers contributed the largest share of sales.
- UPI was the most frequently used payment method.
